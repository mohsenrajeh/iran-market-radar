## 2. Trading Session Phases
- **Pre-Open Auction**: 08:45 – 09:00 IRST
- **Continuous Trading**: 09:00 – 12:30 IRST
- **Closing Auction (TAL)**: 12:30 – 12:45 IRST (where applicable)

---

## 3. Price Limits & Tick Sizes
- **Standard TSE Equities**: $\pm 5.0\%$ of previous session closing price.
- **IFB Base Market**: $\pm 3.0\%$ or $\pm 1.0\%$ (depending on color tier).
- **Equity ETFs**: $\pm 10.0\%$.
- **Tick Size**: $1$ Rial for prices $\le 10,000$ Rials; $10$ Rials for prices $> 10,000$ Rials.
- **Settlement Cycle**: $T+2$ business days.
